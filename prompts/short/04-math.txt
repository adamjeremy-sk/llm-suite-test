A water tank holds 1,200 litres and starts with 300 litres in it. At time 0, inlet pipe A (40 litres per minute) and drain C (15 litres per minute) are opened. Ten minutes later, inlet pipe B (25 litres per minute) is also opened. All three stay open until the tank is exactly full. How many minutes after time 0 is the tank full, and how many litres in total have left through drain C by then? Show your working step by step, then give both answers on a final line.
